Waypoint 01 / 11

Introduction

Media Recovery — branded in‑app as "Restora" ("the app," "we," "us") — helps you find and recover recently deleted photos, videos, audio files and documents from your Android device's Recycle Bin and from folders you choose, and lets you move selected files into a private, PIN‑protected Vault on your device. The app has no user accounts and no sign‑in. Scanning, recovery and the Vault work entirely on your device.

This Privacy Policy explains what the app stores on your device, what integrated Google services may collect automatically for advertising, consent, remote configuration and purchases, and the choices available to you. It applies to the app as distributed on Google Play and is effective as of 1 September 2026. By installing or using Media Recovery (Restora), you agree to the practices described here.

Waypoint 02 / 11

Information we collect

The app collects no personal information and runs no developer server. It stores data only on your device, and the Google services it uses for ads, consent, remote configuration and billing collect a limited set of data automatically as part of their normal operation.

Recovery data (on‑device)

When you scan, the app reads file details — name, type, size, source folder and dates — for items in your device's Recycle Bin and in folders you select, and lists what can be recovered. This information stays in a local database on your device and is never uploaded.

Vault contents (on‑device)

Files you move into the Vault are copied into the app's private storage on your device and, where the system allows, removed from their visible location. These file copies never leave your device.

Security settings (on‑device)

Your Vault PIN and your security‑question answer are stored only as one‑way SHA‑256 hashes — the app cannot read the original values. Optional biometric unlock is handled by Android; the app never accesses your fingerprint or face data.

Preferences (on‑device)

Selected language, dark‑mode and notification toggles, onboarding status, last‑scan times, whether you've seen a permission explainer, and — only if you grant it — the folder location you picked for WhatsApp media.

Advertising data

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your Advertising ID, IP address, device and app signals, coarse (IP‑derived) location, and ad interaction data such as impressions and clicks to serve and measure ads.

Consent, config & purchases

Google's User Messaging Platform records your ad‑consent choice. Firebase Remote Config uses a Firebase installation identifier and basic device/app info to deliver configuration values. Google Play Billing handles the optional subscription and shares only purchase tokens and subscription status.

What we do not collect

The app has no accounts or sign‑in, and it does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts or calendar. It does not use the camera or microphone. Your recovered files, your Vault contents and your photos are never transmitted to us or to any third party.

Waypoint 03 / 11

How we use information

  • Recovery — scanning your device's Recycle Bin and folders you choose, listing recoverable files, and restoring the ones you select.
  • Vault — moving selected files into private on‑device storage and protecting them with your PIN, security question and optional biometric unlock.
  • Preferences — remembering your language, theme, notification choice, onboarding status and recent scan activity so the app behaves consistently.
  • Advertising — showing ads in the free version through AdMob, subject to your consent choices where applicable.
  • Remote configuration — using Firebase Remote Config to tune ad placements and behaviour (for example, enabling or disabling a placement) without shipping an app update.
  • Premium — selling and restoring the optional "Restora Pro" subscription through Google Play and hiding ads while it is active.

Waypoint 04 / 11

Third‑party services & data sharing

We don't sell your information and we don't operate a backend. The app integrates a small set of Google services (through the open‑source Revenue Ads Library wrapper). Each provider receives only the data it needs to do its job and handles it under its own policy.

Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK)

Serves and measures ads in the free version. May process your Advertising ID, IP address, device/app signals, coarse location and ad interactions. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)

Shows the ad‑consent form in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland and records your choice so it can be respected and changed later. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Firebase Remote Config

Delivers configuration values that adjust app behaviour without an update. Uses a Firebase installation identifier and basic device/app metadata. Governed by Firebase's privacy information and Google's Privacy Policy.

Google Play Billing

Processes the optional subscription and manages purchase state. All payment details are handled by Google Play — the app never receives or stores card data, only a purchase token and subscription status. Governed by the Google Play Terms of Service.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or where necessary to protect the rights, property or safety of our users or others.

Waypoint 05 / 11

App permissions

The app requests only what its features need; several network and advertising permissions are added automatically by the Google SDKs. You can review and revoke permissions at any time in Android Settings.

  • Photos & media (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_AUDIO; on Android 12 and older, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) — to read items in the device Recycle Bin and folders you select so they can be listed, and to write recovered files back to your device.
  • All files access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) — optional. Only used for the deep "filesystem sweep" that looks in legacy trash and thumbnail folders the media library doesn't index. You can decline it; the Recycle Bin and folder‑based recovery still work.
  • Access to a folder you choose (Android's Storage Access Framework) — used only when you point the app at your WhatsApp media folder. The grant lasts until you revoke it in Android settings.
  • Biometric (USE_BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINT) — optional fingerprint or face unlock for the Vault, handled entirely by Android.
  • Internet & network state (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) — used only by the Google SDKs (ads, Remote Config, Billing). The app's own scanning, recovery and Vault features never use the network.
  • Advertising ID and ad‑services (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID, Android Privacy Sandbox ad‑services permissions, READ_BASIC_PHONE_STATE) — added by the ads SDK for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention.
  • Billing (com.android.vending.BILLING) — for the optional subscription.
  • WAKE_LOCK / FOREGROUND_SERVICE — used by SDK components to complete short background tasks reliably.

The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, call log or location permissions.

Waypoint 06 / 11

Advertising & your consent choices

The free version of Media Recovery shows ads served by Google AdMob — an app‑open ad on launch and when you return to the app, occasional full‑screen ads at certain transitions, and a native ad on the language screen. Ads are configured remotely, so individual placements may be turned on or off over time.

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, a Google consent form is shown before any personalised ads. You can choose personalised or non‑personalised ads; your choice is saved and is re‑applied on future launches. Where the app exposes a "privacy / consent options" control, you can reopen the form to change your choice.
  • Anywhere, you can limit ad personalisation in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (reset or delete your Advertising ID).
  • Subscribing to Restora Pro removes all ads. The subscription (weekly or yearly) is billed through your Google Play account and renews automatically until you cancel it in Google Play. Use "Restore purchases" on the premium screen to re‑apply an existing subscription.

Waypoint 07 / 11

Storage, security & retention

Recovery details, Vault file copies, security settings and preferences are stored in the app's private storage on your device (a local database and app preferences). There is no cloud storage and no developer database.

Files you restore without vaulting are written to a public Downloads/RecoveredMedia folder on your device so other apps can open them — that is an ordinary file on your device, not a transfer to us.

Cloud backup is disabled for this app (android:allowBackup="false"): your Vault, recovery data and settings are not included in Google Drive backups and do not carry over automatically when you reinstall or switch devices.

The Vault PIN and security‑question answer are stored only as one‑way SHA‑256 hashes. All network communication made by the Google SDKs uses HTTPS/TLS.

Retention. On‑device data stays until you delete files in the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall. Data handled by Google — advertising identifiers, Firebase installation data and purchase records — is retained under Google's own policies.

Waypoint 08 / 11

Your rights & choices

Wherever you live, the app gives you direct control. Depending on your region (for example, GDPR in the EEA/UK or the CCPA/CPRA in California) you may also have statutory rights to access or delete personal data held about you.

Delete your data by removing recovered files, emptying the Vault, clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling.

Change or reset your Vault PIN using your current PIN, or recover access with your security question.

Revoke storage access — turn off All‑files access or a folder grant in Android settings at any time; recovery simply skips what it can no longer read.

Control ad personalisation via the in‑app consent form (where shown) and Android's Ads settings.

Manage the subscription in Google Play (Subscriptions), and use "Restore purchases" in the app for eligible purchases.

Change language or theme at any time from the app's Settings screen.

For data the app stores, the in‑app controls above are the fastest way to exercise your rights. For data held by Google (advertising, Firebase, purchases), use Google's account and privacy tools, or contact us and we will help direct your request.

Waypoint 09 / 11

Children's privacy

Media Recovery (Restora) is a general‑purpose utility, is not directed at children under 13, and is not part of Google Play's "Designed for Families" programme. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will take appropriate action.
